# Public Claims Register

## SAFE_PUBLIC_CLAIMS

- This is a portfolio rebuild derived from graduate forecasting coursework.
- The project demonstrates quarterly seasonal forecasting with two baselines and three parsimonious candidate models.
- Evaluation uses expanding-window rolling origins; no random train/test split is used.
- Automated assertions require every training period to precede every forecast target period.
- On the authorized 12-observation internal demonstration series, seasonal mean is the best-performing model within the evaluated design.
- The workflow produces a four-quarter point forecast; no publicly defensible prediction interval is claimed.
- The short, three-cycle series makes model comparisons statistically fragile and limits external validity.
- Raw and derivative-data rights remain unresolved, so the public interface uses an explicit user-supplied-data workflow and withholds the validated case's exact metrics, forecasts, and data-derived figures.

## TECHNICALLY_SUPPORTED_INTERNAL_CLAIMS_HELD_FOR_RIGHTS_REVIEW

Exact model metrics, robustness values, and course-series point forecasts are supported by the internal validation evidence but are not approved for public export until derivative rights are resolved.

## PROHIBITED_OR_UNSUPPORTED_CLAIMS

- The model is production-ready, production-deployed, or operationally validated.
- The method is universally optimal or proven superior outside this demonstration.
- The project generated business savings, revenue, service improvements, or company impact.
- The historical data are public, licensed for redistribution, self-created, or owned by the portfolio author.
- The target has a specific physical or commercial unit, industry, company, or calendar range.
- The earlier empirical residual bands are validated prediction intervals or have demonstrated coverage.
- The historical coursework grade, instructor feedback, or spreadsheet output constitutes independent model validation.
- The four rolling origins provide broad statistical generalization.

Any future publication must recheck these claims after the data and code license decisions in final Module 8B.
